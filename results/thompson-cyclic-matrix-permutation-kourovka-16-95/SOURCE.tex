\bmp \textbf{16.95.}
{\it Conjecture:\/} If $F$ is a field and
$A$ is in $GL(n,F)$, then there is a permutation matrix $P$ such
that $AP$ is cyclic, that is, the minimal polynomial of $AP$ is
also its characteristic polynomial. \hf \raisebox{-1ex}{\sl
J.\,G.\,Thompson}
