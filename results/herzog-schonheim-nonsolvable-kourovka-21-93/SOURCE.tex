\bmp \textbf{21.93.} (M.\,Herzog, J.\,Sch\"onheim). Let $G$ be a group and let $k \geq 2$. Let $H_1,\dots,H_k$ be subgroups of $G$, and $g_1,\dots,g_k$ elements of $G$ such that the cosets $g_1H_1,\dots,g_kH_k$ form a partition of $G$. Is
it true that $|G:H_i| = |G:H_j|$ for some $i \neq j$?

\makebox[15pt][r]{}This is known to be true for groups with a Sylow tower (M.\,A.\,Berger, A.\,Felzen\-baum, A.\,Fraenkel, {\it  Fund. Math.}, {\bf  128}, no.\,3 (1987), 139--144). Also cf. 20.99.

\hfill \raisebox{-1ex}{\sl L.\,Margolis}
